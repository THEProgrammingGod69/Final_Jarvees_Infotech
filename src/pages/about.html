01Our story

Our story

Jarvees began in Pune with a simple observation: businesses needed SAP done right, and graduates needed a real path into SAP careers. The same experienced consultants could solve both problems.

Our academy was already training learners by mid-2019, and in October 2019 we incorporated as Jarvees Infotech Private Limited. Today the company runs two arms under one roof: Jarvees Infotech, an ERP and SAP consulting practice, and Jarvees Academy, a training and placement-assistance centre.

Clients get a consultant network with strong industry backgrounds and a proven SAP implementation methodology. Learners get those same consultants in the classroom, teaching from real projects rather than slides.

02Purpose

Our goal, mission and vision.

Our goal

To become the number-one SAP education provider by offering the best classroom and online training, and by raising the benchmark on post-training services: placement support, career advice and continuous upskilling through regular industry and academia interaction.

Vision

To be Pune's most trusted bridge between enterprise SAP work and the people who deliver it, where every learner trains on real projects and every client gets consultants who have done the job.

Mission

To deliver practical SAP, CRM and data skills through consultant-led training and live projects, and to deliver SAP implementation, migration and support to businesses with less customisation and fair pricing.

Objectives

  • Support every eligible learner with structured placement assistance
  • Keep the curriculum current with S/4HANA and cloud releases
  • Give every batch at least one live-project experience
  • Grow the consulting practice alongside the academy
  • Maintain ISO 9001 quality standards across both arms
