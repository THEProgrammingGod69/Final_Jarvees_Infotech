What we collect

The enquiry form

When you use the enquiry form, your browser assembles your message on your own device. Nothing is sent to or stored on our web server. You then choose how to send it:

We use the details you send only to reply to your enquiry and to share information about the course or service you asked about. We do not sell or rent personal information.
