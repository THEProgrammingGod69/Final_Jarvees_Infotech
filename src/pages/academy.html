06FAQ

Questions learners ask us most.

Do you offer online classes?

Yes. Every course runs in both online and classroom modes, and batches and timings can be customised for students and working professionals.

Who are the trainers?

Working consultants with at least 10 years of industry experience, who bring real project scenarios into every session.

Do I need an IT background to learn SAP?

No. SAP functional modules such as FICO, MM, SD and HCM are open to IT and non-IT graduates. Technical tracks such as ABAP and BASIS suit learners with a computing background.

Do you provide placement assistance?

Yes, 100% placement assistance for eligible learners: career advice, resume and interview preparation, and support with openings. We assist with placement; we do not sell job guarantees.

Will I get an experience letter?

Freshers who complete an internship project receive an experience letter. It is offered with programmes such as software testing; ask our counsellors which courses include it.

Do you help with academic projects?

Yes. We guide BE, BTech, MCA and BCA students on their academic projects.

How much do the courses cost?

Fees depend on the course and the mode you choose. Contact us for the current fee and batch schedule, and we will share a clear breakdown before you enrol.
